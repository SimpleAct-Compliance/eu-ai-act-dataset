# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First release. Legal status: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), in force since 27 July 2026.

- 33 obligations with article, addressee and application date
- 10 application dates from 2024 to 2030, as JSON, YAML and iCalendar (English and German)
- Annex III: 8 areas, 25 use cases with example and boundary
- Article 5: 10 prohibited practices, including points (ba) and (bb) applicable from 2 December 2026
- Article 99: three fine tiers
